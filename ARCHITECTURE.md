# TAHI and Archivarius

## Purpose

TAHI is an exact multidimensional indexing layer. Archivarius is the multimodal knowledge-ingestion and provenance layer built above it.

## Principles

- Canonical Store is the single source of truth.
- Derived indexes and snapshots are reproducible.
- Exact predicate filtering is separated from semantic extraction.
- Every claim carries provenance, confidence, and status.
- Raw objects remain addressable by stable content hash.

## Layers

1. Sources and ingestion.
2. Canonical Store.
3. Entities, concepts, claims, and relations.
4. TAHI exact indexes.
5. Search, explanation, and verification.

## Scope

Archivarius may ingest dictionaries, encyclopedias, mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, text, code, images, audio, video, and structured data. TAHI indexes typed features and metadata; it does not define the truth of extracted semantic features.
